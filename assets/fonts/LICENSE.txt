Both files were subset with fontTools/pyftsubset to the glyphs used by this site
(Latin + the Chinese characters present in the pages). If new copy introduces
characters outside the subset, regenerate:

  pyftsubset NotoSansSC-VF.ttf --text-file=<all site text> --flavor=woff2 \
    --output-file=assets/fonts/noto-sans-sc-vf.woff2 --no-hinting

The fonts may not be sold on their own; they are embedded here only to render
this website.
